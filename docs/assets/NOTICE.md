# Figure and recording credits

- `game408-cover.webp` — Game408 dataset overview from the project's paper.
- `game408-catalog.webp` — Game408 training and evaluation game catalog from the paper's supplementary materials.
- `gamehunter-pipeline.webp` — GameHunter training pipeline from the project's paper.

The playtest frames reproduce excerpts from recordings of The Darkside Detective, The Abandoned Planet, Orwell: Keeping an Eye On You, The Legend of Skye, and Lucy Dreaming. The hero is cropped from a recording of The Legend of Skye. The reference actions and scene summaries are based on the corresponding Game408 annotations.
